How to trade a row — the candle-3 order
OrderA buy-stop at the Buy stop price — one cent above the high of the 09:45–10:00 ET green candle. It fills only if price breaks the sneaky candle’s high during candle 3.
Window09:00–09:15 CT (10:00–10:15 ET). Cancel anything still unfilled at 09:15 CT. Cancel it early if price trades below the Stop before it fills — the setup is broken.
StopLow of the 09:30 red candle, on every share. Attach it as soon as the buy-stop fills.
Target 1The projected move: the median excursion this much headroom has produced, about a third of the way to yesterday’s range high. A limit sell for half the shares (the larger half on an odd count — the Shares column shows the split).
Target 2Yesterday’s range high. A limit sell for the other half. When Target 1 fills, move the stop on the remaining shares up to your Buy stop price — from then on the trade cannot lose.
ExitFlat by the close on whatever is still open.
SizeEnter today’s cash and your base risk — what you would lose on a Normal trade — above the list. Each row’s R:R scales that base: Full 1.5× for R:R 0.60 and up, Normal 1× for 0.45–0.60, Light 0.75× below; the Size column shows the resulting % and $. Shares = that risk ÷ (Buy stop − Stop), so a stop-out costs the Risk $ shown. Reward $ shows two lines: Target 1 alone (the rest stopped out at entry) and both targets. R:R is measured to Target 1 (marked cash-capped when your cash, not the risk, set the size). If the day’s orders together would need more than your cash, every row shrinks by the same factor — risk keeps its tier ratio and the total never exceeds what you have. A lone order your cash cannot fully cover becomes all-in.
Momentum0–100 percentile within today’s list; the list is sorted by it. It ranks probability, not profit — use it to choose between rows.

08:45 CT · The stalkFirst 15-minute candle (09:30–09:45 ET) closes. Keep every liquid US stock whose candle is red, whose low broke under the previous day's range low, and whose range is at least 0.75× its 14-day ATR — a dramatic break, not a drift.
09:00 CT · The strikeSecond candle (09:45–10:00 ET) closes. Keep the names where it is green (body ≥ 5% of its range), its low never went below the red candle's low, the red candle held the swing low, the headroom to yesterday's range high is above 0 and under 9%, the projected reward is at least 0.35× the risk, and the setup scores at least 3 of the 5 checklist points.
Checklist · 3 of 5One point each. Reclaim: the green candle closes at least 0.1 ATR back above yesterday's range low. Prior up: yesterday closed up. Shallow: the red candle broke yesterday's low by no more than 0.25 ATR. RSI 45: RSI(14) after the green candle is 45 or more. Solo: no more than a quarter of the day's stalk names printed a sneaky candle — a stock-specific flush, not the whole market. The Points column shows each row's score.
Entry · candle 3Buy-stop one cent above the green candle's high, live 09:00–09:15 CT only.
StopLow of the initial red candle. Always.
TargetsHalf at Target 1 — the buy-stop price plus the projected move for its headroom (median excursion, fitted). Then move the stop on the rest to entry and sell it at Target 2, yesterday's range high. Flat by the close otherwise.
RankingMomentum score, a 0–100 percentile within the day's list. Shallower breaks of the range low break ties. Stops under 0.5% from entry are tagged tight stop, not removed.
News checkThe last 48h of headlines are read by model. Published: quiet (no headlines) and clear (generic headlines that explain nothing). Held back: caution (a company-specific event — earnings, downgrade, deal, litigation) and flagged (offering, guidance cut, failed trial, fraud probe, going concern). Drops with a reason tend to keep going.
