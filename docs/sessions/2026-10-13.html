Keep the names where it is green (body ≥ 5% of its range), its low never went below the red candle's low, RSI(14) after it is ≥ 40, the red candle held the swing low, and the headroom to yesterday's range high is above 0 and under 9%, and the projected reward is at least 0.35× the risk.
Entry · candle 3Buy-stop one cent above the green candle's high, live 09:00–09:15 CT only.

TargetBuy-stop price plus the projected move for its headroom (median excursion, fitted). Flat by the close otherwise.
