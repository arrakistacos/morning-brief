The setup

Long only — cash account, no shorting, no options.

08:45 CT · The stalkFirst 15-minute candle (09:30–09:45 ET) closes. Keep every liquid US stock whose candle is red and whose low broke under the previous day's range low. The shorter the lower wick the better — that wick is the stop.
09:00 CT · The strikeSecond candle (09:45–10:00 ET) closes. Keep only names where it is green and its low never went below the red candle's low. That is the sneaky candle: the drop hit resistance.
EntryClose of the green candle.
StopLow of the initial red candle. Always.
TargetIf the red candle broke below the previous day swing low → target is the previous day range low. If it broke the range low but held above the swing low → target is the previous day range high.
RankingSorted by risk/reward, best first. Setups whose stop lands inside the spread (<0.5% or <8% of the red candle's range) are held back as hair-trigger — the ratio is arithmetically true but not executable.
News checkEach candidate's last 48h of headlines is pulled and triaged. A dilutive offering, a guidance cut, a failed trial or a fraud probe means the bounce is a red herring, whatever the candle says.
